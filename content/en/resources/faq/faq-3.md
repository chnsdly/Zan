As a baseline, perform routine visual and thermal checks monthly or quarterly, and conduct a more complete inspection annually. Oil-immersed units usually require periodic oil testing, while dry-type units typically focus on cleaning, torque checks, and ventilation path integrity.

Critical facilities with high utilization or harsh environments may need tighter cycles and condition-based monitoring. Following the manufacturer manual and local utility standards is essential for final maintenance scheduling.
