title = "How do I choose between an oil-immersed transformer and a dry-type transformer?"
description = "Selection depends on installation environment, fire-safety requirements, and lifecycle cost priorities."
date = 2026-04-22T09:00:00Z
publishDate = 2026-04-22T09:00:00Z
+++

If the installation is indoors with strict fire-safety and low-maintenance requirements—such as commercial buildings, hospitals, or data centers—a dry-type transformer is often preferred. It avoids insulating oil management and is generally easier to deploy in occupied areas.

For outdoor utility yards, heavy industrial loads, and projects that prioritize high capacity with strong overload capability, oil-immersed transformers are usually more economical over long-term operation. The right choice should be validated against local codes, ambient conditions, and expected load profile.
