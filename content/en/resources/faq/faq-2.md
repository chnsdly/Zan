+++
title = "What capacity margin is reasonable when sizing a transformer?"
description = "A moderate margin is safer than either aggressive undersizing or excessive oversizing."
date = 2026-04-22T09:05:00Z
publishDate = 2026-04-22T09:05:00Z
+++

In many industrial and commercial projects, a planning margin around 15% to 25% above stable demand is a practical starting range. This helps absorb seasonal variation, short peaks, and near-term expansion without causing chronic overload.

However, oversizing too much increases no-load losses and reduces efficiency at normal operating points. Capacity should be finalized with real interval load data, harmonics review, and thermal verification under the worst realistic duty cycle.
