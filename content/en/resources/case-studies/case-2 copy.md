## Project Background
A 120 MW photovoltaic project required robust step-up transformation across multiple inverter blocks. The owner needed high thermal endurance, low no-load loss, and reliable operation in hot, dusty conditions.

## Solution
The EPC selected sealed oil-immersed transformers with corrosion-resistant outdoor configuration:

- Optimized impedance matching for inverter output characteristics.
- Adopted on-load monitoring for oil temperature and load profile.
- Implemented phased commissioning to shorten grid-connection risk windows.

## Results
After the first operating season, the project team observed:

- More stable daily generation profile under high irradiance.
- Lower thermal stress during peak summer dispatch.
- Improved grid compliance and reduced reactive-power adjustment events.

## Key Takeaway
For renewable energy projects, transformer selection should align with both environmental conditions and inverter behavior to maximize long-term yield and grid compatibility.
