On-Load Tap-Changing Oil-Immersed Transformer

The on-load tap-changing oil-immersed transformer performs tap switching under load, enabling online voltage regulation during system voltage fluctuation and load variation to maintain bus stability and improve delivered power quality.

Request a Quote

Product Details

This series integrates an on-load tap changer, allowing tap-position transitions without interrupting service or disconnecting load. When utility voltage swings or downstream demand shifts cause output deviation, the transformer can regulate voltage online to keep the bus within target limits, supporting continuous supply and more stable end-user power quality.

It is widely applied in industrial and utility distribution systems where voltage-quality requirements are strict and load variation is frequent. Proper engineering selection should consider regulation range, tap steps, switching duty, insulation level, and temperature-rise performance together so that voltage-control capability, mechanical endurance, and field reliability remain aligned with real operating conditions.
